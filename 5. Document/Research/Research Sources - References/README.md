# PARIKSHAK — Research Sources & Statutory References

## 📌 Statutory & Regulatory Framework

PARIKSHAK's curriculum, safety rules, and digital certification criteria comply with Indian national safety mandates and international heavy industrial safety guidelines.

---

## 1. Indian Mining & Industrial Safety Frameworks

1. **Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS)**
   - *Mines Act, 1952*: Statutory provisions for safety, health, and welfare of workers in coal, metalliferous, and oil mines.
   - *Coal Mines Regulations (CMR), 2017*: Regulations 129–145 governing flame-proof equipment, ventilation limits, methane monitoring, and emergency escape routes.
   - *DGMS Technical Circular No. 04 of 2019*: Guidelines on multi-lingual safety training and digital tracking of mine personnel competence.

2. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**
   - **IS 2190:2010**: Selection, installation, and maintenance of first-aid fire extinguishers in industrial premises.
   - **IS 2878:2004**: Specification for portable fire extinguishers (Carbon Dioxide type).
   - **IS 4308:2003**: Dry chemical powder for fire fighting.
   - **IS 15683:2018**: Portable fire extinguishers performance and construction standards.

3. **Factories Act, 1948 & State Factory Rules**
   - Section 38: Precautions in case of fire, mandatory drills, and certified clear escape passageways.
   - Section 41: Special provisions for hazardous processes, toxic gas exposure limits, and worker health monitoring.

---

## 2. International Standards & Technical Guidelines

1. **OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration)**
   - *29 CFR 1910.157*: Portable fire extinguishers in workplace settings (placement, inspection, and maintenance).
   - *29 CFR 1910.147*: Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout - LOTO) procedures and zero-energy state verification.
   - *29 CFR 1910.146*: Permit-required confined spaces (atmospheric testing, forced ventilation, and attendant responsibilities).

2. **NFPA (National Fire Protection Association)**
   - *NFPA 10*: Standard for Portable Fire Extinguishers (classification, distribution, and discharge ratings).
   - *NFPA 70E*: Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace (arc-flash boundaries and energized work restrictions).
   - *NFPA 652*: Standard on the Fundamentals of Combustible Dust.

3. **NIOSH (National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health)**
   - NIOSH Pocket Guide to Chemical Hazards (PEL, REL, STEL, and IDLH concentrations for mine gases).
   - Ergonomics and Augmented Reality in Workforce Training technical reports.
